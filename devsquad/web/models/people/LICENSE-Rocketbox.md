# The people in the 3D office

The eight people (`Male_Adult_*.glb`, `Female_Adult_*.glb`) and their animations (`anim_m.glb`, `anim_f.glb`)
are converted from the Microsoft Rocketbox avatar library, https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox,
which Microsoft publishes under the MIT License below.

What changed in the conversion: FBX to glTF binary, textures resized to 1024 px (hair to 512 px), and the
animation clips cut to at most 14 seconds at 10 to 20 frames a second, with each loop blended back to its first frame.
Clips used: idle_neutral_01, walk_neutral_01, sit_table_breathe_01, sit_chair_idle_relaxed_01,
gestic_talk_neutral_01, gestic_listen_neutral_01, gestic_presentation_left_01, drink_idle, cell_phone_textmessage
(male and female versions).
