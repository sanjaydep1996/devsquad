## Pick the method from the question
tags: research, method, user research, discovery
when: users, customers, why, understand, research, discover, need
decide: Use interviews to learn why (5-8 people), usability tests to check a design (5-8), surveys to measure how many (100+), card sorting for navigation (15-30), diary studies for behaviour over time (10-15) and A/B tests to compare two versions.

## Run a short interview guide
tags: interview, guide, questions
when: interview, talk to users, feedback, customers
decide: Warm up (5 min), learn their current workflow (10), go deep on the topic (20), show the concept (10), wrap up (5). Ask about real past moments, not hypotheticals.

## Test the design before building it all
tags: usability, prototype, test
when: design, flow, onboarding, checkout, ui, redesign
decide: Put a clickable prototype in front of 5 target users with realistic tasks; fix what most of them stumble on before writing the full feature.

## Synthesize into decisions
tags: synthesis, affinity, insights, jobs to be done
when: research, feedback, insights, findings
decide: Group observations into themes (affinity mapping), state each as an insight with evidence, and rank fixes on an impact and effort matrix.

## Define success before shipping
tags: metrics, success, experiment, hypothesis
when: launch, feature, experiment, growth, conversion
decide: Write the hypothesis and the metric that would prove it wrong before building; check it after launch.
